## Useless (remove)

- **Role** on the sheet: a select that only picked which KPIs show. The KPIs stay: crit value, crit rate / crit damage, and the game's third. (builds)
- **"Make these the game's defaults"** on the KPI tiles. Default targets stay where they are stored, but the button goes. (builds)
- **"More details: the game's own sheet"**: a second, older sheet with less in it. (builds)
- **Build names that grow** ("Arlecchino (2) (2) (2)"): a new build is "Build 2"; the name field is an optional label. (builds)
- **Preview** in Plan farming: the materials show as you change the levels. (goals)
- **The curve past hard pity**: the second run after a lost 50/50 goes. (pulls)
- **Source labels on your own records**: MANUAL, admin, feed, "typed in here". (copy)
- **Manifest, catalog and live cells** on the games overview. (library)

## Lacking (add)

- **Select mode**: a click on a card selects it; it no longer opens the character. (characters)
- **Element colours as in each game**: Endfield's Electric is yellow; ZZZ's Physical is yellow and its Electric blue; WuWa's Havoc is crimson. (characters)
- **Filters as icons**: element, weapon type and rarity as toggle chips; Sort stays a list. (characters)
- **The default build's weapon on each card**: its icon, level and refinement. (characters)
- **A character opens on its default build**: switch builds with tabs, make another the default. (builds)
- **A Weapons screen**: every weapon you own, with level and refinement, who wields it, and adding from the catalog. (weapons)
- **A picker**: a field you type in, with a list of icons to scroll and click, for sets, weapons and characters; native dropdowns opened off their field. (picker)
- **Correcting a logged pull**: change the pity a 5★ came at, won or lost, and which unit; delete it. (pulls)
- **Banner history**: past banners with your pulls on each and the 5★ you got; featured characters open their pages. (pulls)
- **Goals**: click a goal to open it; a character goal shows its steps (each ascension phase, each talent's target); link goals (a weapon's farm under its character's). (goals)
- **Wishlisting an event or a banner**, so "Only what I wishlisted" keeps it. (wishlist)
- **Endgame**: exact reset times, and the team used on each floor, side or boss. (endgame)
- **Games overview**: characters owned / total, limited pulls, stamina, open goals and the backlog per game. (library)
