## Storage (the answer to "propose me a free solution")

**Cloudflare R2** stays the pick (ADR 0006, the mirror script is ready): free tier 10 GB storage and **no egress fees**, far past what six games' art needs. Georges creates the bucket and sets the four `R2_*` secrets plus `VITE_ASSET_BASE` (DEPLOY.md §7); `npm run assets:mirror -- --game <key>` then copies every catalog key's art into our own store and the app stops depending on the community CDNs. Until then the app hotlinks those CDNs (already wired, with drawn fallbacks everywhere). Alternatives considered: Vercel Blob (egress-metered, small free tier) and Supabase Storage (1 GB, 2 GB egress/month) — both tighter than R2 for images.
