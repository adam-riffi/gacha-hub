## Decisions (Georges: "take every decision")

- **Characters:** the unit URL stays and shows the default build in place, so the links from banners, the timeline and the pull history keep working. Owned reads the ownership alone, so a try reads Not owned. A default target left as it was is no longer saved as the build's own.
- **Gear (A):** the chosen piece's column always holds a piece (the first shown until one is picked), so nothing opens or moves; editing a piece's stats stays on its wearer's sheet; adding and farming stay in the set's panel, opened from the column or the picker.
- **Gear filters:** a game's sets are grouped by the slots they fit only when they come in kinds; a slot's chip shows a piece's art where the catalog has art per slot (Genshin); a picked main stat that the picked slots cannot have stops filtering instead of emptying the list.
